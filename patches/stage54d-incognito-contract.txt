Stage54d native incognito contract:

1. Toggle android.view.inputmethod.EditorInfo.IME_FLAG_NO_PERSONALIZED_LEARNING (0x01000000).
2. Invoke Gboard InputBundleManager/Look.i() (InputBundle.reactivateIme).
3. Reactivation re-runs active IME onActivate(EditorInfo, incognitoMode, keyboardType) using Leqy.G()/Looh.G().
4. AbstractIme stores incognito in H and recomputes M (learning enabled); AbstractIme.o() returns false when H is true.
5. Publish the normal Lowq.e(...) InputSessionNotification.
6. mlq.hn delegates the normal input-view update to native mlq.d, allowing the stock mlr.c HEADER_MENU incognito SoftKeyDef/icon to represent the same real state.
7. No toast is used.
